The app will show the current stock check here. Planning and printing never use up or reserve supplies.

  • Consumables: total needed for all activities in the week.

This proposal does not calculate inventory or make stock-availability claims.

Review controls & limits

All rooms and activities here are fictional. The proposal keeps drafts in memory while switching room, week or view. Refreshing resets everything.

Staff preview shows the last saved plan. Approval/publishing, live stock checks, server permissions and version conflicts are not simulated. These remain explicit review or implementation requirements.
